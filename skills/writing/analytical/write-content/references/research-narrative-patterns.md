# Research Narrative Patterns

> Last updated: 2026-10-02

Consult this guide during section planning when the history of an idea helps establish the
argument. Allocate space to the relevant discoveries, then draft that selected arc. Research
narrative is optional; other sections may need only a brief citation.

## Core principle

Research citations can be **chronological narrative beats** that trace intellectual evolution, not just authority markers. Choose the few contributions needed for the inference; there is no citation-count target. A coherent arc must preserve disagreements and cannot imply that chronology proves a claim.

## Pattern 1: Progressive understanding

Frame research as a journey of deepening insight. The samples use phrases like:
- "经济学家早就在研究'租'，越研究就越感到..." (Economists have been studying rent for a long time, and the more they research, the more they realize...)
- "至此经济学家已经意识到..." (By this point economists had realized...)
- "但学者们在1990年代又有了一次观念跃迁" (But scholars had another conceptual leap in the 1990s)

The pattern: *we thought X → deeper study revealed Y → but then Z forced us to reconsider*.

## Pattern 2: Chronological discovery arc

Sample 1 (经济租) traces 200+ years of intellectual evolution:

```
早在祖师爷亚当·斯密 1776 年出版的《国富论》一书中，就已经直觉地提出...

到了 19 世纪初，古典经济学家李嘉图（David Ricardo）把这个直觉系统化...

然后是 19 世纪末，马歇尔（Alfred Marshall）搞了个观念跃迁...

至此经济学家已经意识到，超额收益最终会沉淀到某种因为稀缺而被占有的位置上。

1967 年，公共选择理论经济学家戈登·塔洛克（Gordon Tullock）提出...

那么 1974 年，国际贸易与发展经济学家安妮·克鲁格（Anne O. Krueger）进一步指出...

至此你可能觉得"租"就是不劳而获……但学者们在 1990 年代又有了一次观念跃迁。

1993 年，战略管理学者玛格丽特·彼得拉夫（Margaret A. Peteraf）提出...

1996 年，两个战略学者，亚当·布兰登伯格和哈伯恩·斯图亚特提出...
```

The narrative arc: intuition (Smith 1776) → systematization (Ricardo 1800s) → conceptual leap (Marshall 1890s) → realization → shift to mechanism (Tullock 1967, Krueger 1974) → seeming conclusion → another leap (1990s) → modern synthesis.

Each citation is a plot beat, not a footnote.

## Pattern 3: Research as specific investigation

Introduce studies as targeted investigations of concrete questions, not abstract authority:

- "一篇 2006 年发表的论文专门研究了心脏外科医生" (A paper published in 2006 specifically studied cardiac surgeons)
- "还有一篇 2008 年的论文发现" (Another 2008 paper found)
- "2005 年的一篇论文，专门研究了医院里的关节置换手术团队" (A 2005 paper specifically studied joint replacement surgery teams in hospitals)

Not "Research shows..." but "A 2006 study examined surgeons and found..."

The concrete framing: *who was studied, what was measured, what they discovered*.

## Pattern 4: Introducing researchers with their insight

Sample 1 introduces researchers with their contribution, not just a citation number:

- "公共选择理论经济学家戈登·塔洛克（Gordon Tullock）提出，'有人拿到租'这件事本身未必造成多大社会危害，真正造成危害的是所有人开始为抢租而浪费资源"
- "英国管理学者珍妮·纳哈皮特（Janine Nahapiet）和印度裔管理学者苏曼特拉·戈沙尔（Sumantra Ghoshal）的一篇论文提出，组织资本的好坏，关键在于你这个公司是怎么让知识流动的"

The pattern: *[relevant context] [researcher] proposed [the insight this argument needs]*.

Include a researcher's field when it explains the analytical lens. Nationality and biography do not establish the scope or validity of a finding; omit them unless substantively relevant.

## Pattern 5: Transition phrases that signal intellectual progression

From Sample 1:
- "至此..." (By this point...)
- "那么..." (And then...)
- "但..." (But...)
- "总结来说..." (In summary...)

From Sample 2:
- "首先你得从个体归因的习惯中跳出来" (First you must break out of the habit of individual attribution)
- "怎么跳出戏曲思维，看见系统呢？咱们可以借鉴安全工程中的学说" (How to break out of theatrical thinking and see the system? We can borrow from safety engineering scholarship)

These phrases signal: accumulation, turning point, synthesis, next layer.

## Pattern 6: Inline citations as narrative beats, not just footnotes

Sample 2 (系统思维) uses inline citations to build momentum:

```
社会学家 C. 赖特·米尔斯（C. Wright Mills）在 1959 年出版的《社会学的想象力》一书中有个说法...

社会心理学家李·罗斯（Lee Ross）在 1977 年提出一个概念叫「基本归因谬误（fundamental attribution error）」...

英国心理学家詹姆斯·瑞森（James Reason）有个说法，比如发生了一起重大事故，你可以有两种视角...
```

Each citation is introduced with context (field, name, year, key concept), then immediately applied to the ongoing argument. The citations feel like witnesses called to testify in order, not a bibliography dump.

## When to use this pattern

- Explanations where successive discoveries clarify a difficult mechanism
- Teaching material that needs both conceptual authority and accessibility
- Arguments where intellectual genealogy strengthens the claim
- A section budget that supports explaining the selected discoveries and their limitations

## When to skip this pattern

- Sections whose budget would be consumed by intellectual history
- Personal essays where lived experience is the primary evidence
- How-tos where procedural clarity matters more than intellectual history
- Arguments where direct evidence supplies the needed warrant without a historical account

## Execution checklist

When choosing and drafting a research narrative:

- [ ] Citations appear in chronological order when tracing concept evolution
- [ ] Each selected contribution has a precise source, a relevant insight and treatment within the section budget
- [ ] Transition phrases signal accumulation, turning points, or synthesis
- [ ] Studies are framed as specific investigations of concrete questions
- [ ] The narrative arc has a beginning (intuition/observation), middle (systematization/mechanism), and current state
- [ ] Each citation adds one distinct layer; no citation is purely decorative
- [ ] The genealogy strengthens the explanation without becoming a literature review

## Example passages for reference

### Opening that establishes progressive understanding (Sample 1)

> 学术界早就在研究"租"，越研究就越感到赚钱就得靠"租"。
>
> 早在祖师爷亚当·斯密 1776 年出版的《国富论》一书中，就已经直觉地提出，土地所有权会吸走高于正常工资和正常利润的剩余。

Translation: *Academics have been studying "rent" for a long time, and the more they research, the more they feel that making money depends on "rent." As early as Adam Smith's 1776 Wealth of Nations, he intuitively proposed that land ownership would absorb surplus above normal wages and normal profits.*

### Mid-section transition signaling conceptual leap (Sample 1)

> 至此你可能觉得"租"就是不劳而获……但学者们在 1990 年代又有了一次观念跃迁。
>
> 1993 年，战略管理学者玛格丽特·彼得拉夫（Margaret A. Peteraf）提出，一家企业要想长期获得超额收益，就得有持续的「竞争优势（competitive advantage）」...

Translation: *By this point you might think "rent" is just unearned income... but scholars had another conceptual leap in the 1990s. In 1993, strategy scholar Margaret A. Peteraf proposed that for a firm to earn sustained excess returns, it must have durable competitive advantage...*

### Research introduced as specific investigation (Sample 3)

> 比如一篇 2006 年发表的论文专门研究了心脏外科医生。按理说做手术是一种高度个人化的技能，可是这个研究发现，那些在自己原本所在的医院做手术非常成功、病人存活率很高的医生，一旦跳槽去了另一家医院，他们的手术存活率就会下降。

Translation: *For example, a paper published in 2006 specifically studied cardiac surgeons. Logically, surgery is a highly individualized skill, but this study found that doctors who were very successful at surgery in their original hospitals, with high patient survival rates, would see their surgery survival rates drop once they switched to another hospital.*

### Closing synthesis (Sample 1)

> 经济学家说"租"，战略学家说"竞争优势"和"附加值"，投资人说"护城河"、"收费桥"和"定价权"，产品人说"卡位"和"入口"……等等等，他们说的其实都是同一件事：你是否占住了一个别人短期内绕不过去、替代不了、复制不来的位置。

Translation: *Economists say "rent," strategists say "competitive advantage" and "added value," investors say "moat," "toll bridge," and "pricing power," product people say "position" and "gateway"... and so on. They're all talking about the same thing: whether you occupy a position that others cannot bypass, replace, or copy in the short term.*
