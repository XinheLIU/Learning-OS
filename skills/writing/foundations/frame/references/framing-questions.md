# Framing Questions

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Use only questions whose answers are missing from supplied material, dialogue and relevant snapshots.
Bring a concrete candidate or interpretation first. The same questions work for article and book scale.

| Uncertainty | Ask | Completion signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Reader relevance | 谁在什么情况下会遇到这个问题？不解决会怎样？ | a recognizable situation and consequence |
| Author stake | 你为什么在意它？哪次经历或观察使它值得写？ | an actual motivation, without invented experience |
| Gain | 读者原来怎样理解？读完多了什么解释、连接、判断或边界？ | a specific before/after in understanding |
| Scope | 这一篇只回答哪一个问题？什么留给下一篇？ | one outcome and meaningful exclusions |
| Judgment | 你现在怎样回答？哪部分仍不确定？ | author's answer or bounded uncertainty |
| Real objection, when arguing | 什么证据或反例会让你改变判断？反对者最有力的理由是什么？ | fair opposition and a testable limit |
| Explanation | 哪一步最难理解？读者已经知道什么可以帮助解释？ | a mechanism or dependency to develop |
| Exploration | 哪几种答案还可能成立？怎样区分它们？ | comparison criteria without a forced winner |
| Continuity | 和上一次快照相比，你改变了什么？下一篇还缺哪个答案？ | a reason to extend, revise or stop |

An occasion can make a familiar question urgent, but a current event is not mandatory. A source's
insight can legitimately be explained to a new audience. A useful review of a book is also a valid
piece when that is the reader promise. Those faithful explanations need reader gain, not a new
synthesis. Original analytical writing additionally needs a defensible contribution beyond source
restatement, developed and tested in its framework checkpoint.

Record author wording verbatim where it carries a judgment or personal incident. Proposals stay
proposals until accepted. Do not repeat a question whose answer is already explicit.
